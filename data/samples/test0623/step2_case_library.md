# SME Credit Approval Case Library

## Case 1 - Approved: Established Manufacturer
- **Industry**: Automotive parts manufacturing
- **Loan Amount**: RMB 2,500,000 (secured by commercial real estate)
- **Operating History**: 8 years
- **DTI**: 45%
- **Cash Flow**: Positive for 14 consecutive months
- **Collateral**: Factory building valued at RMB 4,200,000 (LTV 60%)
- **Decision**: Approved, grade A, pricing base + 2.0%
- **Conditions**: Quarterly financial reporting; LTV monitored annually.
- **Key Reasoning**: Strong operating cash flow, acceptable DTI, adequate collateral, low industry risk.

## Case 2 - Rejected: High DTI Trading Company
- **Industry**: Import/export trading
- **Loan Amount**: RMB 800,000 (unsecured)
- **Operating History**: 3 years
- **DTI**: 72%
- **Cash Flow**: Negative in 4 of the last 12 months
- **Decision**: Rejected
- **Key Reasoning**: DTI exceeds 60% cap; unstable cash flow; unsecured facility for a cyclical trader.

## Case 3 - Escalated: Tech Startup with IP
- **Industry**: Artificial intelligence software
- **Loan Amount**: RMB 1,200,000
- **Operating History**: 18 months
- **Revenue**: RMB 0 (pre-revenue)
- **IP Assets**: 3 granted patents in natural-language processing
- **Decision**: Escalated to Technology Finance Committee
- **Key Reasoning**: Does not meet standard SME cash-flow criteria but has valuable IP assets requiring specialist assessment.

## Case 4 - Approved with Conditions: Growing Retail Chain
- **Industry**: Food and beverage retail
- **Loan Amount**: RMB 600,000 (unsecured)
- **Operating History**: 4 years
- **DTI**: 52%
- **Cash Flow**: Positive for 11 of last 12 months
- **Decision**: Approved, grade BBB, pricing base + 3.0%
- **Conditions**: Personal guarantee from the founder; monthly sales reporting; automatic review if DTI rises above 55%.
- **Key Reasoning**: Slightly below 12-month positive cash flow but strong growth trend and founder guarantee mitigates risk.

## Case 5 - Rejected: AML Alert
- **Industry**: Electronics wholesale
- **Loan Amount**: RMB 1,500,000
- **Operating History**: 5 years
- **Financials**: DTI 38%, positive cash flow
- **AML Signal**: 60% of recent deposits came from unrelated offshore entities
- **Decision**: Rejected
- **Key Reasoning**: Unexplained third-party inflows triggered AML escalation; no satisfactory explanation provided.
