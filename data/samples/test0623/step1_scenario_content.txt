This scenario covers the end-to-end credit approval process for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) applying for working capital loans, project loans, and trade finance facilities ranging from RMB 100,000 to RMB 5,000,000.

Business Boundary:
- Applicant: legally registered SMEs with at least 24 months of operating history.
- Products: unsecured working capital loans (<=RMB 1M), secured loans (<=RMB 5M), and supply-chain financing.
- Exclusions: real estate development loans, personal consumer loans, and government-platform financing.

Target Users:
- Front-line relationship managers (RMs)
- Credit analysts in the risk management department
- Members of the loan review committee

Decision Types:
- Approve (with or without conditions)
- Reject
- Request supplementary materials
- Escalate to the credit committee

Key Evaluation Dimensions:
1. Basic eligibility and compliance screening
2. Financial health and debt service capacity
3. Collateral adequacy and valuation
4. Industry risk and macro environment
5. Credit history and behavioral scoring
6. Fraud and anti-money-laundering (AML) signals

Output Required:
A structured approval recommendation including risk grade, suggested pricing (interest rate and fees), conditions precedent, and monitoring requirements.
